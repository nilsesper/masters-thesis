\vspace{-0.5cm}
The Physics Institute III A at RWTH Aachen University operates a cosmic test stand with a fully functional \ac{DT} chamber and all necessary peripherals, which is similar to the chambers used in the \ac{CMS} experiment. Recently, the chamber was equipped and recommissioned with the readout electronics that will be used in the Phase-2 upgrade of CMS. In addition, a scintillation detector with \ac{SiPM} readout has been constructed at our institute. With the goal of integrating the readout of the scintillator into the chamber backend system, new electronics and corresponding firmware have been developed. These electronics feature the \ac{lpGBT} chip, a serializer-deserializer device developed at \acs{CERN}. It allows to inject the detector data into the \ac{DT} backend system, and maintains synchronization with the global clock. This thesis presents the newly developed scintillator readout chain and first recorded scintillator hit data. This data is compared to the tracks from the \ac{DT} chamber data using a self-implemented triggerless reconstruction algorithm which is based on the \ac{CMS} \ac{DT} local reconstruction. The synchronization of the two detectors could be achieved in this thesis, with a time precision in the $\mathcal{O}(\SI{10}{\nano\second})$ range. The implemented track reconstruction algorithm was successfully validated, using the scintillator hits as a reference.
